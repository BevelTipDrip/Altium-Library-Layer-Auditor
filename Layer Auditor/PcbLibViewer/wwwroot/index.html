Fills in missing documentation from the part's own geometry — by default, a true top-down projection of the embedded 3D STEP model where one exists (Smoothing loosens how closely the traced outline follows small fillets/notches; Simple mode skips the 3D model and falls back to a plain bounding-box/stored-outline shape). Courtyard and assembly outline each replace whatever's currently on their layer; pin-1 only adds a ring, it doesn't clear anything.
